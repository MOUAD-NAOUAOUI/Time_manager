# Business Goals & Target Users

## 1. Business & Product Objectives
1. **Reduce Time Estimation Errors**: Bridge the gap between estimated and actual minutes spent on tasks.
2. **Increase Focus Continuity**: Minimize context switching by providing uninterrupted single-task focus sessions.
3. **Automate Schedule Rebalancing**: When tasks run over or priorities change, automatically reorganize the remaining schedule.
4. **Actionable AI Feedback**: Deliver context-aware insights rather than generic motivational quotes.

## 2. Target User Personas

### Persona A: The Over-Ambitious Planner
- **Profile**: Schedules 12 hours of high-intensity tasks per day; gets discouraged by 2 PM.
- **Pain Points**: Chronic underestimation, schedule collapse, guilt.
- **TimeSpace Solution**: Constraint scheduler enforces realistic rest intervals; AI Coach warns against fatigue-inducing workloads.

### Persona B: The Context Switcher
- **Profile**: Software engineer juggling multiple code reviews and development tasks.
- **Pain Points**: Forgets what was worked on, loses track of time.
- **TimeSpace Solution**: Live session tracker with instantaneous overtime prompts (+5m, +10m, +15m, +30m), preserving task integrity.

## 3. Key Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Session Completion Rate**: Percentage of started sessions that conclude with completed tasks.
- **Estimation Accuracy Delta**: |Estimated Minutes - Actual Minutes| convergence over time.
- **Weekly Hour Map Density**: Visual measure of productive vs. idle hours throughout the week.
